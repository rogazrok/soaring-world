# Version history

## 1.0.0 — Public release

The edited Kanto world and the accumulated flight, discovery and visual work
are packaged as a release. Pre-release fixes preserve legacy rewards, restore
item objects on new repeatable visits, correct music after Hidden Area battles,
restrict development scene entry and release the flight session's haze mesh.
Lance gives the Dragon Call through a manually advanced conversation and walks
off screen afterwards.

## 0.10.33 — Modular bridges and pine trees

Cycling Road became editable bridge pieces rather than a fixed map landmark.
The gallery gained straight spans and a matching 90-degree corner. Pine trees
received a narrower, taller crown and darker foliage. Subsequent work included
Lance dialogue and pre-release bug fixes.

## 0.10.31–0.10.32 — Model polish

Environment models were refined, including the Celadon Department Store,
Cinnabar Mansion, pine materials and Cycling Road geometry. The fixed road was
later superseded by the modular bridge system.

## 0.10.28–0.10.30 — Time of day and map updates

Soaring began following the engine's current day/night lighting. The loading
animation received the same treatment, and the author's edited world map was
synchronized into the playable mod.

## 0.10.27 — Rider motion

Dragonite and Red gained subtle visual banking, wing-linked bobbing and climb
or descent reactions while preserving flight handling.

## 0.10.24–0.10.26 — World rendering and atmosphere

Terrain and water boundaries were cleaned up. Palette experiments led to the
approved palette B. Work added atmosphere effects, animated water, world-space
clouds and mist, a panoramic horizon, textured exterior terrain and sparse
distant scenery. Loading-screen refinements introduced Surf music and a
wing-only waiting loop that ends when the world is ready.

## 0.10.23 — Camera, Lance and Soaring UI

CHASE and HIGH SOAR views, the post-League Lance encounter, visited-town landing
rules and the Soaring Map established the main exploration flow. Start opens
the map while airborne.

## 0.10.21–0.10.22 — Dragon Call cinematics

Dragonite replaced the original bird takeoff sequence. A three-note whistle,
Dragonite's cry and matching landing animation completed the transition into
and out of flight.

## 0.10.19–0.10.20 — Hidden Area content

Five regular Hidden Areas gained distinct layouts, encounters and persistent
discovery. A special encounter area uses its own unlock and one-shot rules.
Boundary polish and rare starter encounters expanded the content.

## 0.10.17–0.10.18 — Rewards and loot profiles

Repeatable visits gained per-instance rewards, saved pickup state and different
loot profiles.

## Earlier development

The initial work established the Kanto flight world, native asset rendering,
town landings, Hidden Area spawning and standalone map editors. Later fixes
refined exit transitions, step counting and Fly compatibility.
